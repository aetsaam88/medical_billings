About Vantage RCM

One team behind your billing, staffing, and online presence

We started as a two-person billing desk for a family clinic. Today we run coding, credentialing, virtual staffing, and marketing for hundreds of practices — built on the same principle: your admin work should never slow down patient care.

Certified coders double-check every claim against payer rules before it ever leaves our desk, cutting denials at the source instead of chasing them afterward.

Claims move through intake, coding, and submission on a same-week cycle, so your cash flow doesn't wait on a backlog.

Every process is built around HIPAA and payer-specific requirements, with audit trails your practice can hand over any time it's asked.
